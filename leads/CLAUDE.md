# Leads folder rules

These rules apply to this folder only.

1. **Everyone here is made up.** The people in `leads.csv` are practice leads for testing. They are not real.
2. **Never add real people or real email addresses.** Not even your own, and not even for a test.
3. **Every row needs a consent value.** Don't add a row with an empty `consent` column.
4. **Never change the @example.com addresses.** That domain is reserved and can't receive mail, which keeps everyone safe. Every new address must end in `@example.com` too.

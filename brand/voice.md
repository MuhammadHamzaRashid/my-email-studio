# Kettlebird Tea – Brand Voice Guide

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, for people on our waitlist.

## Our voice in three words

**Warm, calm, a bit playful.**

- **Warm:** we talk like a friend putting the kettle on for you.
- **Calm:** we never rush anyone. Tea is a slow, gentle thing.
- **A bit playful:** a light joke or a tea pun now and then, but never silly.

We are **never pushy**. We invite people in. We don't chase them.

## Words we use

| Word or phrase | How we use it |
|---|---|
| **Steep** | For making tea, and as a light pun ("Take time to steep"). |
| **Cosy** | For the feeling of a good cup and a quiet moment. |
| **Little ritual** | For the habit of making tea, and for the monthly box. |
| **Steepers** | Our name for customers and Steep Club members. |

## Words we avoid

- "Act now"
- "Hurry"
- "Last chance"
- "Cheap"

These sound pushy or make the tea seem low quality. Say what you mean in a calm way instead. For example, write "Spots in the first box are filling up" rather than "Hurry, last chance!"

## Claims we must never make

1. **No health claims.** Don't say our tea cures, heals, calms stress, helps you sleep or boosts anything. This covers hints too, like "the tea your body needs".
2. **No "organic" unless the tea is certified.** Only call a tea organic if it has organic certification. If you are not sure, leave the word out.

## How we sign off

Every email ends with:

> Happy steeping,
> The Kettlebird crew

# My Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- **brand/** – who the business is: voice, tone, colours, logo and key messages.
- **leads/** – the people and groups we want to reach, and notes about them.
- **campaigns/** – one folder per campaign, with the plan and every email draft.
- **notes/** – research, decisions and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

1. **Write in plain English.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon.
2. **Never send email to real people.** Write drafts and tests only. Do not send anything to a real address, even through Resend.
3. **Ask me before deleting anything.** This includes files, folders and drafts.

# The Steep Club Launch – Campaign Plan

**Status:** Draft for review
**Campaign dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** Email only

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** Your Little Ritual

**In one sentence:** We invite our waitlist to join The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, over four calm weeks of email.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription between 7 December 2026 and 3 January 2027.

That is 15% of a list of about 2,000 people. That is a high target for email, but people on a waitlist have already asked to hear from us, so it is within reach. See Risks (section 9).

**Other goals:**
- Learn which of our three groups each person belongs to, so later emails fit them better.
- Keep unsubscribes low (under 1% per email). The list is worth more than one launch.
- Sell gift subscriptions in time for Christmas.

### A note on timing

The first Monday of next month is **7 December**. That puts the whole launch in the Christmas and New Year season. This helps gift buyers, but it also sets a hard date: gifts must be ordered in time to arrive before Christmas. Gift emails go out early (weeks 1 and 2). We send nothing from 24 to 26 December.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people on our waitlist, in three groups.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might hold them back | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | Like tea but don't know much about it. Usually drink a supermarket teabag. | To try new teas without having to choose. A little guidance. | "Will I like it?" "Is this too fancy for me?" | Thinking about it |
| **Gift buyers** | Want a thoughtful present for a tea lover. | A gift that feels personal and arrives on time. | Delivery dates. Not knowing what the person likes. | Ready to buy, with a deadline |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Drink tea every day and already have favourites. | Variety, good quality, and something to look forward to each month. | "Is it better than what I already buy?" Price for value. | Ready to buy |

**Where they are:** all of them are in our email list. That is the only channel in this plan.

**Do we know who is in which group?** Not yet. The `leads/` folder is empty. Email 1 asks people to tell us with one click (see section 5). Anyone who doesn't click gets the general version of each email.

---

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Every month, a box of good tea arrives at your door: a little ritual that's just for you.

**Supporting messages:**

| Message | Best for | Proof we need |
|---|---|---|
| We choose the teas, so you get to enjoy them. No guesswork. | Beginners | What is in the first box, with tasting notes. |
| A gift that keeps arriving, month after month. | Gift buyers | Gift options, delivery dates, how the gift note works. |
| Teas you won't find on the supermarket shelf. | Daily drinkers | Where the teas come from and how we choose them. |
| Easy to pause or cancel any time. | Everyone | Our real pause and cancel policy. |

**Rules for every email** (from `brand/voice.md`):
- Warm, calm, a bit playful. We invite. We never chase.
- No "act now", "hurry", "last chance" or "cheap".
- No health claims, not even hints. Talk about taste, smell and the cosy moment, not what tea does to your body.
- Only say "organic" for teas with organic certification.
- Call members **Steepers**.
- Sign off with: *Happy steeping, The Kettlebird crew*

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** | These people asked to hear from us. It costs very little and we can track every click. | 8 launch emails (some with a version for each group), plus a welcome email for new members. | Medium | Almost all of it: our time, plus the email tool. |

**Not in this plan:** paid ads, social media, PR. If we fall short by week 3, a simple social post or two would be the lowest-cost next step. That is optional.

**Sending rules:**
- **Stop the launch emails for anyone who subscribes.** They get the welcome email instead.
- No more than two emails a week to any one person.
- Nothing on 24, 25 or 26 December.
- Every email gets a test send to our own inbox first. **No sending to real people until the plan and drafts are approved.**

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **15% is a high target.** We may fall short. | Check every Monday. If we're behind by week 2, try a launch offer or a stronger week 4 email to the daily drinkers (most likely to buy). |
| **Christmas inboxes are full.** Our emails may get lost. | Keep subject lines warm and clear. Send early in the week. Skip 24–26 Dec. |
| **Gift orders miss the delivery date.** | Confirm courier cut-off dates by 4 Dec. Put the order-by date in every gift email. Decide on a gift card or January gift as a back-up. |
| **Most people don't click a group button.** | Send them the general version. Ask again in E4 with a single question. |
| **Too many emails cause unsubscribes.** | Two a week at most. Stop emails once someone subscribes. Watch the unsubscribe rate after each send. |
| **A health claim or pushy word slips in.** | Check every draft against `brand/voice.md` before review. |

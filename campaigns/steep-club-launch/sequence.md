# The Steep Club Launch – 4-Email Sequence

**Status:** Draft for review. Nothing sent.
**Who gets it:** Everyone on the waitlist (about 2,000 people).
**Goal:** 300 new Steep Club subscriptions by Sunday 3 January 2027 (see `plan.md`).

The plan lists up to 7 launch emails. This sequence keeps the 4 that matter most. The gift emails (E3 and E4-G) are folded into Emails 2 and 3, and the seasonal emails (E5 to E7) are folded into Email 4.

The email files are in `emails/`. Words in [BRACKETS] are facts we still need to confirm.

## The story across the four emails

1. **Hello, we're open.** What the club is and how it works.
2. **Here's what you'd get.** The real teas, so it feels concrete.
3. **Here's why it's easy to say yes.** Clear answers, no small print.
4. **Here's a small, cosy reason to start now.** The new year and the first-box date. Then we stop.

## Rules for the email tool

- **Exit:** anyone who subscribes or buys a gift leaves the sequence at once and gets the welcome email (`email-welcome.md`).
- **Gift buyers:** someone who buys a gift for another person also leaves the sequence. They may still want a box for themselves later, but we don't chase.
- **Don't send to:** anyone who has unsubscribed, or who has emailed us with a problem in the last 2 days.
- **No re-entry:** once the sequence ends, people go back to our normal emails.
- **Replies to Email 1:** tag each reply as *beginner*, *gift* or *daily* by hand, or with a simple rule in the email tool. We can use the tags for later campaigns.

## A/B tests

1. **Email 1 subject.** Test "The kettle's on: The Steep Club is open" against "Your seat at The Steep Club is ready". Split 10% / 10% of the list, then send the winner (by opens after 4 hours) to the other 80%.
2. **Email 4 button.** Test "Start my little ritual" against "Join The Steep Club". Split 50 / 50. Winner is the one with more subscriptions, not just clicks.
3. **Email 2 send time.** Morning (8am) against evening (7pm). Split 50 / 50. Measure clicks.
